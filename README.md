## Home Page

1. Hero section

- Company name and slogan.
- A short description of what the company does.
- A prominent button leading to Services or Products.
- A large image or illustration.

2. About section

- A short introduction to the company.
- A button leading to the About page.

3. Services overview

- Three or four service cards.
- Each card has an icon, title, and short description.
- A button leading to Services.

4. Featured products

- A few product cards with images, names, and prices or descriptions.
- Links to individual Product Details pages.

5. Latest news

- Two or three recent news cards.
- Links to the News page and individual articles.

6. Team preview

- A few team members with portraits and positions.
- A link to the Team page.

7. Call to action

- Something like "Have a project in mind?"
- A button leading to Contact.

8. Footer

- Navigation links.
- Contact information.
- Social media links, if appropriate.
